# Voice commands

These commands take the **fast path**: they are recognised on your computer without calling any AI,
so they answer in a fraction of a second, work offline and cost nothing. Everything else goes to your
brain, so you can phrase it however you like.

You can start with "Jarvis", "Hey Jarvis" or "Ok Jarvis" (Italian: "Ehi Jarvis"). Punctuation and
accents don't matter.

| Intent | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|
| Stop speaking | "stop", "quiet", "be quiet", "shut up" | "basta", "zitto", "silenzio", "fermati", "ferma" |
| Stop everything | "stop everything", "cancel all" | "ferma tutto", "annulla tutto", "interrompi tutte le sessioni" |
| Status | "status", "how's it going?", "what's running?" | "a che punto sei?", "a che punto siamo?", "come sta andando?", "cosa sta girando?", "stato" |
| Repeat | "repeat", "say that again" | "ripeti", "puoi ripetere", "come hai detto" |
| Time | "what time is it?", "what's the time?" | "che ore sono?", "che ora è?" |
| Date | "what's the date?", "what day is it?", "what day is today?" | "che giorno è oggi?", "che data è?" |
| Open settings | "open settings", "settings" | "apri le impostazioni", "impostazioni" |
| Private mode on | "local mode", "private mode" | "modalità locale", "modalità privata", "tutto in locale" |
| Private mode off | "private mode off", "turn off local mode" | "disattiva la modalità privata", "esci dalla modalità locale" |
| Timer | "timer for 10 minutes", "set a timer for half an hour", "1h 30m countdown" | "timer di 10 minuti", "metti un timer di un'ora e mezza", "conto alla rovescia di venti minuti" |
| Named timer | "timer 9 minutes for the pasta" | "timer di 9 minuti per la pasta" |
| Alarm | "alarm at 7:30", "wake me up at 6 am" | "sveglia alle 7:30", "svegliami alle 7 e 30" |
| Reminder | "remind me in 20 minutes to call Marco" | "ricordami tra 20 minuti di chiamare Marco" |

Durations understand digits and words ("ten", "twenty", "half an hour", "dieci", "mezz'ora",
"un'ora e mezza"), seconds, minutes and hours.

## Everyday

| Intent | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|
| Morning briefing | "good morning" (the whole sentence) | "buongiorno" |
| Start dictation | "dictate", "start dictation", "take dictation" | "dettatura", "detta", "avvia la dettatura" |
| Stop dictation | "stop dictation", "cancel dictation" (or **Esc**) | "fine dettatura", "annulla la dettatura" |
| What you know about me | "what do you know about me?" | "cosa sai di me?", "cosa ricordi di me?" |
| Forget today | "delete today's history", "forget today" | "cancella la cronologia di oggi", "dimentica oggi" |
| Copy the last result | "copy it", "copy the result" | "copialo", "copia il risultato" |
| Shopping list: add | "add milk, eggs and bread to the shopping list" | "aggiungi il latte e le uova alla lista della spesa" |
| Shopping list: read | "what's on the shopping list?" | "cosa c'è nella lista della spesa?" |
| Shopping list: remove | "remove eggs from the shopping list" | "togli le uova dalla lista della spesa" |
| Shopping list: clear | "clear the shopping list" | "svuota la lista della spesa" |

Requests that mention what you **copied** ("copied", "clipboard" / "copiato", "appunti") or your
**screen** ("on my screen", "what am I looking at", a short "explain this error" / "sullo schermo",
"spiegami questo errore") are recognised locally and answered by your brain with that content attached
as data. Screen questions need **Screen access** turned on.

## Approvals by voice

Only for **low and medium** risk. High risk always needs a click.

| Answer | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|
| Yes | "yes", "yeah", "yep", "sure", "ok", "okay", "go ahead", "do it", "allow" | "sì", "certo", "vai", "procedi", "ok vai", "fallo", "consenti", "va bene" |
| No | "no", "nope", "don't", "do not", "deny", "stop", "cancel" | "non farlo", "annulla", "nega", "lascia stare", "ferma" |

"Please" / "pure" / "grazie" at the end are fine.

## What Jarvis says back

Fast-path replies are fixed phrases, for example *"Timer set for 10 minutes."* / *"Timer di 10 minuti
avviato."*, *"Okay, in 20 minutes I'll remind you to call Marco."*, *"Private mode on. Everything stays
on this computer."*

For everything else, see [What Jarvis can do](/everyday/features).
