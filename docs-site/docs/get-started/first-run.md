# First run

The welcome wizard has five short steps. You can go back at any time and change everything later in
**Settings**.

::: steps
1. **Welcome**
   A big orb and one sentence. Click **Get started**.

2. **Microphone**
   Jarvis explains why it needs the microphone and makes a promise: *the wake word runs on your
   computer*. Click **Allow microphone** and speak — the level meter proves it works.

3. **Choose your brain**
   Four cards: **Continue with ChatGPT** (uses your Plus/Pro plan, no API key), **Use Claude Code**
   (shows whether it is found and signed in), **Use an API key** (Anthropic or OpenAI), **Run locally**
   (Ollama). Connect one or more; one is marked **primary**. [Details →](/guides/brains)

   ![Onboarding: choose your brain](/assets/screenshots/onboarding-brain-dark.png)

4. **Voice**
   Pick ElevenLabs, Fish Audio, OpenAI, Local (Piper) or System, press ▶ to preview, and choose
   **English** or **Italiano**. [Details →](/guides/voices)

5. **Try it**
   Say *"Jarvis, what time is it?"*. The pill appears, Jarvis answers, a little celebration plays
   (disabled if you prefer reduced motion). Click **Finish**.
:::

## What happens next

- Jarvis lives in your **menu bar / system tray**. Close the Home window; it keeps listening for the
  wake word.
- The **Home** window shows suggestion chips. Click one, or say what's under it.
- The tray menu gives you: Home, Sessions, Mute microphone, Private mode, Settings, Quit.

## Good first settings

| Where | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| General | Launch at login | Jarvis is ready when you are |
| General | Your name | "Good morning, Lorenzo" |
| Shortcuts | Push-to-talk | Hold to speak without the wake word |
| Agents & projects | Add a project folder | For developer work, with a permission level |
| Privacy | History retention | 30 days by default; 0 keeps nothing |

::: callout info "Nothing connected yet?" icon:info
Jarvis always starts. Without a brain it still handles the fast path — time, date, timers, alarms,
reminders, stop, private mode — and tells you what is missing for everything else.
:::
